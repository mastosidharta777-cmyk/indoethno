# IndoEthno — Artist → Place Paths V1

**Pilot:** Yogyakarta  
**Purpose:** Keep the music-led discovery layer specific. Each artist must lead to a verified place relationship before broader cultural and travel context is introduced.

## Rule

Do not attach the same generic Yogyakarta attractions to every artist page. The sequence is:

**Sound → verified Place relationship → artist-specific Culture context → broader destination layer → Travel Intent**

A place relationship can be citywide when the evidence is genuinely about place identity; it does not have to be a venue. Broader travel context must be labeled as editorial/travel context rather than implied as a direct artist relationship.

## Jogja Hip Hop Foundation

- **Place:** Yogyakarta, citywide.
- **Why:** Indonesia’s Ministry of Culture describes JHF as a soundtrack of Jogjakarta life and documents the mix of urban hip-hop, Javanese language, gamelan, traditional verse and literature.
- **Culture path:** Javanese language & literature → hip-hop as social culture in Yogyakarta.
- **Primary source:** https://kebudayaan.kemdikbud.go.id/ditwdb/jogja-hip-hop-foundation-profile-music-europalia-arts-festival-2017/
- **2026 research context:** https://digilib.uin-suka.ac.id/id/eprint/76027/

## Gayam16 / Yogyakarta Gamelan Festival

- **Place:** Plaza Pasar Ngasem.
- **Why:** DIY Tourism records Yogyakarta Gamelan Festival 31 at Plaza Pasar Ngasem, 21 July–2 August 2026, organized by Komunitas Gayam16.
- **Culture path:** living/adaptive gamelan → Ngasem/Kraton travel context.
- **Primary source:** https://visitingjogja.jogjaprov.go.id/44560/yogyakarta-gamelan-festival-2026-21-juli-2-agustus-2026/

## Tembang Gula

- **Place:** Jogja National Museum.
- **Why:** ARTJOG 2026 lists Tembang Gula’s 28 June performance; ARTJOG visitor information places the 2026 program at Jogja National Museum.
- **Culture path:** current Javanese-language dangdut → contemporary Yogyakarta arts ecosystem.
- **2026 agenda:** https://artjog.id/2026/agenda.php?lang=en

## Replacement gate

Do not replace an artist merely to chase reach. Replace only when at least one of these fails:

- an official/authorized media source cannot be used;
- current activity cannot be verified;
- the artist cannot support a credible Sound → Place → Culture → Travel story.
